# Battle sound auditions v1

Original synthesized PCM WAV prototypes generated for Sokudoku. No source audio samples or Trickster recordings used. 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit. Files: dash, punch/jelly/hybrid single hits, corresponding 480 ms charge combos, and miss-combo. The contact starts at 160 ms in successful combos. Peaks capped at 0.8 full scale. Audition page starts at 50% playback volume and never autoplays.

Candidates were procedurally synthesized from filtered noise, pitch envelopes and layered transient tones. These are listening prototypes, not yet wired into gameplay. Original reference archive: https://sounds.spriters-resource.com/pc_computer/trickster/asset/394370/ . Alternative CC0 pack researched: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds (download returned HTML, no pack files included).
